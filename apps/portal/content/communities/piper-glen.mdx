## On the course

TPC Piper Glen brings the TPC pedigree to south Charlotte — an Arnold Palmer
signature design and a private membership club at the heart of the neighborhood.

## The lifestyle

One of south Charlotte's first master-planned golf communities, Piper Glen has
aged into wide, canopy-shaded streets and established homes. It is a settled,
quietly upscale place to live within the city limits.

## Location & access

Off Rea Road near I-485, Piper Glen is about 20 minutes from uptown and minutes
from the StoneCrest and Blakeney shopping districts.
