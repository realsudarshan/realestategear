## On the course

Tega Cay Golf Club plays across a peninsula on Lake Wylie, with water in view on
much of the round — an accessible course that doubles as the town's social hub.

## The lifestyle

Tega Cay ("beautiful peninsula") is built into the Lake Wylie shoreline, with
beaches, marinas, and parks woven through the neighborhoods. South Carolina
property taxes and the Fort Mill school district make it a value draw for buyers
crossing the state line.

## Location & access

Just over the NC line in York County, Tega Cay is about 25 minutes from uptown
Charlotte via I-77 and minutes from the Baxter and Kingsley shopping districts.
