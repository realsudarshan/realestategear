## On the course

Longview Country Club is a Jack Nicklaus signature design — a marquee layout that
gives this Waxhaw community a destination-golf reputation.

## The lifestyle

Behind the gates, Longview is custom homes on larger estate lots with a full
clubhouse, pool, and tennis. It appeals to buyers who want signature golf and
privacy without leaving the Charlotte metro.

## Location & access

In Waxhaw at Charlotte's southern edge, Longview is roughly 30–35 minutes from
uptown, with the shops and historic downtown of Waxhaw close by.
