## On the course

The community is built around a championship course on a peninsula of Lake
Norman — dramatic elevation changes and water carries make it one of the most
talked-about layouts in the region.

## The lifestyle

The Point is gated and amenity-rich: a full marina, lakeside dining, tennis, and
fitness. Architecture skews to larger custom and waterfront estate homes, drawing
buyers who want privacy and resort-grade amenities close to home.

## Location & access

Set in Mooresville at the northern reach of Lake Norman, The Point is about 30
miles from uptown Charlotte, with Davidson and Cornelius nearby for everyday
shopping and dining.
