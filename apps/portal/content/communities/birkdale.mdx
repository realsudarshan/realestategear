## On the course

Birkdale Golf Club is an Arnold Palmer-designed public course threading through
the neighborhood — accessible golf that anchors the community without a private
membership gate.

## The lifestyle

Birkdale is the approachable, family-friendly end of Lake Norman golf living.
Tree-lined streets and a community pool sit a short walk or drive from Birkdale
Village, an open-air center of shops, restaurants, and a movie theater.

## Location & access

In Huntersville off I-77 exit 25, Birkdale is roughly 20 minutes north of uptown
Charlotte and a quick hop to the lake itself.
