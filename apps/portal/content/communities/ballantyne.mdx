## On the course

Ballantyne Country Club is a private 18-hole layout at the center of one of south
Charlotte's most recognized addresses, with a full clubhouse, tennis, and aquatics.

## The lifestyle

Inside the gates, the neighborhood is custom and estate homes on generous lots, a
short drive from the Ballantyne business and dining district. It draws executives
and families who want club living with city convenience.

## Location & access

At Charlotte's southern edge near the South Carolina line, Ballantyne offers fast
access to I-485 and a roughly 25-minute drive to uptown or the airport.
