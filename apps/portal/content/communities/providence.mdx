## On the course

Providence Country Club is a private 18-hole course with a family-focused club
culture — golf alongside a strong swim, tennis, and social calendar.

## The lifestyle

The neighborhood pairs golf-community living with some of Charlotte-Mecklenburg's
most sought-after schools, which keeps demand steady and the streets full of
families. Homes range from traditional two-stories to larger custom builds.

## Location & access

Along the Providence Road corridor in south Charlotte, the community is close to
Waverly and Ballantyne dining and an easy reach of I-485.
