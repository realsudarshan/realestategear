## On the course

The Peninsula Club wraps a mature, water-touched layout around the Cornelius
shoreline. It is a private, members-and-residents club at the center of one of
Lake Norman's most established golf neighborhoods.

## The lifestyle

This is lake life with a club at its heart: a marina and boat slips, tennis and
swim programs, and a calendar of member events. Homes range from townhomes and
patio homes to large waterfront estates, so the community spans first move-ups to
forever homes.

## Location & access

Cornelius sits about 20 miles north of uptown Charlotte off I-77, with Birkdale
Village's shops and restaurants minutes away and Charlotte Douglas International
roughly 35 minutes south.
