## On the course

Firethorne Country Club anchors the community with a private 18-hole course,
clubhouse dining, and a busy swim-and-tennis program.

## The lifestyle

Firethorne is a Union County favorite for families chasing space and schools: the
Marvin-area attendance zone is among the strongest in the region, and lot sizes
run larger than comparable in-town neighborhoods.

## Location & access

In Marvin near the Waxhaw line, Firethorne is about 30 minutes from uptown
Charlotte with quick access to the Ballantyne employment hub just north.
